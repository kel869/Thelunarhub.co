Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use The Lunar Hub, we may collect your name, email address, and details shared in inquiry or booking forms. We do not collect payment information directly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to facilitate connections between you and readers, respond to inquiries, and improve our platform. We will never sell your personal information.

3. Sharing with Readers

When you submit a booking inquiry, your name, contact details, and session request will be shared with your chosen reader solely to facilitate your session.

4. Session Confidentiality

All communications during a reading session are intended to remain private between you and your reader. The Lunar Hub does not access or record session content.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold. Contact us at houseovirgo@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Lunar Hub is for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

1. Information We Collect from Readers

When you apply to join as a reader, we collect your name, display name, email, social handles, bio, specialty, and years of experience to evaluate your application and build your public profile.

2. Public Profile Information

Once approved, your display name, bio, specialty, photo, and booking link will be publicly visible in our directory.

3. How We Use Reader Information

We use your information to manage your listing, facilitate client connections, and communicate platform updates. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Client Inquiry Data

When a client submits an inquiry to you, we forward their name, email, session type, and message. You must handle this information confidentially.

5. Data Retention

We retain reader profile data for as long as you remain active on the platform. Upon removal, your public profile will be taken down promptly.

6. Your Rights as a Reader

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of any personal information we hold. Contact us at houseovirgo@gmail.com.
